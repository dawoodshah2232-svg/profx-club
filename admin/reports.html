Reports

Signup conversion, verification, activation, attendance, retention, acquisition cost.

Metric definitions (from the spec)

Registration conversion
Completed new registrations ÷ unique signup-page visitors (same period). Visitor tracking is a production integration.
Verification rate
Email-verified members ÷ completed registrations.
Activation
Verified members who book a class or access a resource within 7 days.
Attendance
Attendees ÷ confirmed bookings.
Retention
Activated members returning for a meaningful activity within 30 days.
Acquisition cost
Attributable campaign spend ÷ new verified members from that campaign.
