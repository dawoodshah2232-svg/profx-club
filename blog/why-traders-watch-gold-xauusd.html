Gold Trading

Why Traders Watch Gold (XAUUSD)

September 06, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

XAUUSD — gold priced in US dollars — moves further in a day than many currency pairs move in a week. Here is why it commands so much attention.

A market with memory

Gold has been valued for thousands of years, and that history gives it unique behaviour: it rallies in crises, trends powerfully, and respects big round numbers.

Traders watch it as both an opportunity and a barometer of global fear.

Bigger moves, bigger lessons

A typical gold day can swing $20–$40 — each dollar on a standard lot is $100.

That leverage-like punch from the instrument itself means beginners must use smaller sizes than they expect. Gold forgives nothing and teaches position sizing fast.

Liquidity around the clock

Gold trades nearly 24 hours on weekdays with deep liquidity, especially during London and New York sessions.

You can almost always get in and out — but 'can trade anytime' tempts overtrading, so keep your fixed sessions.

Start by watching

Before trading gold, watch it for a month: note how it moves around news, how far it travels daily, and how your demo emotions react.

Respect earned through observation beats lessons bought with losses.

Key takeaways

  • Gold moves fast — size positions smaller than forex instincts suggest.
  • It trends well and respects big levels; crises lift it.
  • Watch for a month on demo before trading it live.

Frequently asked questions

What does XAUUSD mean?

XAU is gold's code, USD the dollar — the price of one troy ounce of gold in dollars.

Is gold riskier than forex?

Its daily ranges are larger, so the same lot size risks more. Adjust size down accordingly.

When is gold most active?

London and New York sessions, especially around US economic data.
