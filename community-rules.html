Community rules

The rules that keep our Telegram and WhatsApp spaces safe.

1. Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech or abusive conduct. Disagreements stay civil.

2. No impersonation

Never pose as ProFX Club staff, educators or other members.

3. No selling in DMs

Unsolicited direct-message selling or pitching leads to removal.

4. No guaranteed returns

Nobody may promise profits, 'risk-free' trades or guaranteed signals.

5. No password or fund requests

Staff will never ask for passwords, seed phrases or money. Report anyone who does immediately.

6. No spam

Keep discussion on topic. No referral-link spam, no cross-posting.

7. Report & block

Use your platform's report/block tools, then escalate to a moderator.

8. Enforcement

Breaches lead to warnings or removal. Removal decisions are documented. Appeals go to the support channel.

9. Voluntary

Joining any group is voluntary. Registration never adds you automatically. Version: v0.1-demo — owner-approved text pending.
