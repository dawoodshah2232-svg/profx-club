Weekend Routine: Review Like a Pro

August 06, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

Weekends are when traders improve without market pressure. One focused hour beats five distracted ones.

Review the week's trades (20 min)

Open your journal: how many trades followed the plan? What were the three costliest mistakes?

What worked? Write one paragraph summarising the week — forcing it into words reveals what numbers hide.

Score your process (10 min)

Grade yourself A–F on: plan adherence, risk discipline, emotional control.

Process grades predict future results better than this week's profit. Celebrate an A-process losing week; interrogate a C-process winning week.

Study one concept (20 min)

Pick one thing — divergence, session behaviour, a currency's drivers — and study it deeply: articles, historical charts, notes.

Weekly deep dives compound into genuine expertise within a year.

Plan the week ahead (10 min)

Note high-impact events, mark key levels on your pairs, and set your session times.

Monday-you will thank Sunday-you. Then close everything and rest — recovery is part of the routine.

Key takeaways

  • Weekly review: journal, process grades, one deep study topic.
  • Plan next week's events, levels and sessions in 10 minutes.
  • Rest deliberately — recovery is part of professional routine.

Frequently asked questions

How long should the routine take?

About an hour. Consistency beats marathon sessions.

What if I had a terrible week?

Especially then — the review converts pain into lessons. Be factual, not self-attacking.

Should I backtest on weekends?

Excellent use of weekend time — quiet markets, zero pressure, pure learning.
