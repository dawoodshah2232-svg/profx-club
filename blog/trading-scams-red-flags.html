Trading in UAE

Common Trading Scams and Red Flags

August 17, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

Scammers love beginners because beginners want shortcuts. Learn the playbook so you never become the victim.

Guaranteed returns

Anyone promising fixed monthly profits from trading is lying — markets cannot guarantee anything.

This single red flag identifies most scams: 'earn 10% monthly, guaranteed', 'risk-free trading', 'double your money'. Real trading involves real risk of loss.

Signal sellers and bots

Expensive signals or 'AI robots' with screenshots of luxury lifestyles are marketing, not trading.

If their system printed money, they would trade it — not sell it for $99/month. Legitimate educators teach skills; scammers sell dreams.

Fake brokers and account managers

Clone websites of real brokers, unlicensed firms that 'can't process your withdrawal yet', and Telegram 'managers' who trade your account for a fee (then vanish).

Verify licences independently and never give anyone your account password.

Protecting yourself

Check regulation on official registers, never send crypto to strangers, be sceptical of lifestyle marketing, and ask in trading communities — 'has anyone heard of X?' Scams hate sunlight.

If it feels urgent and secret, walk away.

Key takeaways

  • Guaranteed profits = guaranteed scam. No exceptions.
  • Never share account passwords or send money to 'managers'.
  • Verify everything independently; ask communities before trusting.

Frequently asked questions

Are all signal services scams?

Most paid ones are. Free community trade discussions for learning are different from paid 'guaranteed' signals.

Someone DM'd me profits screenshots — legit?

Screenshots are trivially faked. Treat unsolicited DMs as hostile until proven otherwise.

I think I've been scammed — what now?

Stop sending money, save all evidence, report to your local authorities and the relevant regulator.
