Community operating model

One-way channel for class announcements, webinar reminders and educational content. No noise, no selling.

A separately moderated discussion space for peer questions and networking, staffed when moderators are available.

Offered by language or city only when there are enough members and moderators. Your phone number's visibility depends on the platform's settings — check before joining.

Banned: impersonation, unsolicited DM selling, guaranteed-return claims, abusive conduct, and any request for passwords or funds.
