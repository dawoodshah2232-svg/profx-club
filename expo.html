Frequently asked questions

When and where is the next ProFX Expo?

Dates and venue are to be announced. Join the club free and you will be notified when passes open.

Which pass should I choose?

Visitor is free and covers the exhibition floor. Trader adds the trader lounge, strategy hub and clinic. Premium adds all workshops and priority seating. VIP adds the awards gala seat, networking dinner and concierge.

Can I use a discount code on passes?

Yes — valid sample codes such as EXPO25 or EARLYBIRD20 reduce the illustrative price at checkout in this demo. No payment is taken.

Is the ProFX League part of the expo?

Yes — the league arena heats and grand final take place live on the expo floor. Enter the league here.

Do I need to be a club member to attend?

Anyone can get a pass, but club members get member-only discount codes and event notifications. Join free.
