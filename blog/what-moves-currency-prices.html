Forex Basics

What Moves Currency Prices? Key Drivers

September 27, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

Currencies move because money flows toward opportunity and safety. These are the fundamental forces that push exchange rates around.

Interest rates

Central bank interest rates are the biggest driver. Higher rates tend to attract foreign investment seeking better returns, which can strengthen a currency.

When traders expect a rate hike, they often buy the currency in advance — and sell if the hike disappoints. Watch central bank calendars closely.

Inflation and economic data

Inflation reports, jobs numbers and GDP growth shape rate expectations.

Strong data can lift a currency; weak data can sink it. Markets react in seconds to major releases like US non-farm payrolls, so beginners should avoid trading directly through big news until they understand the volatility.

Risk sentiment

In uncertain times, money flows to 'safe haven' currencies like the US dollar, Swiss franc and Japanese yen, while riskier currencies fall.

When confidence returns, the flow reverses. This risk-on/risk-off rhythm drives many of the market's biggest weekly moves.

Politics and surprises

Elections, trade disputes and geopolitical shocks can move currencies fast.

Unlike scheduled data, surprises cannot be planned for — which is exactly why stop-losses and sensible position sizes exist. No analysis predicts everything.

Key takeaways

  • Interest-rate expectations are the dominant currency driver.
  • Big data releases cause fast moves — beginners should stand aside.
  • Risk sentiment shifts money between safe and risky currencies.

Frequently asked questions

Which news matters most for forex?

Central bank decisions, inflation data and US jobs reports typically move markets most.

Can I predict news outcomes?

No — and professionals often get them wrong too. Focus on managing risk around news, not predicting it.

Do technical traders ignore fundamentals?

Smart ones don't. Even chart traders avoid holding positions through major announcements.
