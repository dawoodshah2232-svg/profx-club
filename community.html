How it works

Community operating model

Telegram announcements

One-way channel for class announcements, webinar reminders and educational content. No noise, no selling.

Discussion space

A separately moderated discussion space for peer questions and networking, staffed when moderators are available.

WhatsApp groups

Offered by language or city only when there are enough members and moderators. Check your number's visibility before joining.

Zero tolerance

Banned: impersonation, unsolicited DM selling, guaranteed-return claims, abusive conduct, requests for passwords or funds.

Report & block

Use your platform's report/block tools, then escalate to a moderator. Removal decisions are documented.

Voluntary, always

You choose which spaces to join. Free education never requires joining any chat.
