Had a problem with a broker?

We help you understand your options: complaint preparation, due-diligence resources and — only with your explicit consent — referrals to qualified professionals.

Scope

What we can and can't do

✅ Club support can

  • Review your issue and its urgency privately
  • Provide complaint-preparation checklists and templates
  • Share broker due-diligence and fraud-awareness resources
  • Offer referral to a qualified professional — only with your explicit consent

🚫 We do not

  • Promise fund recovery or any legal outcome
  • Provide court representation
  • Collect passwords, seed phrases or payment-card details
  • Share your case with anyone without your written consent
Club support is not a professional service. Referrals to lawyers or dispute professionals are separate engagements with their own terms — we only introduce you after you approve the sharing in writing.
Process

How a request works

Submit privately

Members submit a private request from their dashboard: category, country, provider, brief facts, dates, what help you need.

We review

Support reviews scope and urgency, asks only for necessary records, and replies privately in your dashboard.

Information or referral

You get information, or we offer a professional referral. Nothing is shared externally without your explicit consent.
