Economic Calendars: Never Miss Key Events

August 10, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

An economic calendar lists upcoming data releases that move markets. Checking it daily is a five-minute professional habit.

How to read one

Events are ranked by expected impact (often colour-coded).

Each shows the previous value, forecast, and actual result on release. High-impact events — central bank decisions, inflation, jobs data — deserve your attention; low-impact ones can be ignored.

Why beginners should care

High-impact releases cause spreads to widen and prices to whip violently for minutes.

New traders holding positions through them get stopped out by noise. The calendar lets you choose: stand aside, reduce size, or observe and learn.

The daily routine

Each morning, note today's high-impact events and their times in your timezone (Dubai = GMT+4).

Plan trades around them — many traders simply avoid new entries 15 minutes before and after major releases.

Learning from releases

Watch a few releases on demo without trading: observe the spike, the reversal, the spread widening.

This education is free and permanently changes how you respect news. MT5 even has a calendar built in.

Key takeaways

  • Check the calendar daily; note high-impact events in Dubai time.
  • Avoid new positions around major releases as a beginner.
  • Watch releases on demo to learn their behaviour safely.

Frequently asked questions

Which events matter most?

Rate decisions, CPI/inflation, NFP/jobs data, and GDP — plus central bank speeches.

Where do I find a calendar?

ForexFactory, Investing.com, and most broker portals offer free ones; MT5 has one built in.

Should I ever trade the news?

Experienced news traders exist, but beginners should observe first — news spikes are unforgiving.
