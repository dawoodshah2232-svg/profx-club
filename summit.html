Frequently asked questions

How is the summit different from the expo?

The expo is the large public gathering with an exhibition floor. The summit is a smaller, senior, B2B-focused edition built around executive panels and closed-door roundtables.

Who should attend the summit?

Brokerage executives, IB and affiliate leaders, fintech founders, liquidity providers, compliance professionals and institutional traders.
