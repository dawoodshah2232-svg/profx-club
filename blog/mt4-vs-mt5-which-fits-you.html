MT4 vs MT5: Which Platform Fits You

August 13, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

MT4 and MT5 dominate retail forex. They look similar, differ under the hood, and the choice matters less than brokers claim.

The headline differences

MT5 is newer: more timeframes, more order types, an integrated economic calendar, and better backtesting.

MT4 is older, simpler, and has the larger library of third-party indicators and robots. For manual beginners, both do the job.

Who should pick MT4

If your broker, educator or strategy resources are MT4-based, stay there — compatibility beats novelty.

Its simplicity is genuinely beginner-friendly, and decades of tutorials exist for every button.

Who should pick MT5

If you want more timeframes, netting/hedging options, or better strategy testing, MT5 is the modern choice.

New traders with no legacy attachments often start here and never look back.

What actually matters more

Your broker's reliability, your risk management, and your strategy dwarf the platform choice.

Pick whichever your learning path uses, learn it deeply on demo, and stop second-guessing. Platforms don't make traders.

Key takeaways

  • MT5: more features and better testing. MT4: simpler, huge resource library.
  • Match your platform to your broker and learning materials.
  • Broker quality and risk habits matter far more than MT4 vs MT5.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch from MT4 to MT5 later?

Yes — skills transfer quickly; only custom indicators/robots need conversion.

Are MT4/MT5 free?

Yes, through brokers. Never pay anyone for the platform itself.

Do I need anything else besides MT4/MT5?

A journal and an economic calendar cover most beginners' remaining needs.
