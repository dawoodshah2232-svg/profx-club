Privacy notice

How ProFX Club collects, uses and protects member data.

1. What we collect

Name, email, and — only if you choose — city, country, experience, language, interests and WhatsApp number. We never ask for broker credentials, account balances or identity documents for ordinary registration.

2. Why

To run your membership: verification, event bookings, class reminders and community access. Service emails (verification, welcome, booking confirmations) are required for membership.

3. Marketing consent is separate

Promotional email and WhatsApp marketing each need your explicit opt-in. You can join and stay a member without either. Opt-out anytime in your profile — it never cancels your membership.

4. Sharing

We never sell member lists. Your data is not shared with sponsors or partners without your specific authorisation. Assistance requests are shared externally only with your written consent.

5. Retention

Retention periods are set by the owner and published here before launch (owner input pending).

6. Your rights

Access, correct or request deletion of your data from your Profile page at any time.
