Gold Trading

XAUUSD Sessions: Best Times to Watch

September 03, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

Gold's personality changes by session. Trade it when it is awake; leave it alone when it naps.

Asian session: quiet

Tokyo hours are usually gold's calmest — tight ranges, low volume.

Fine for observation, poor for momentum strategies. Breakouts here often fail when London arrives.

London: the wake-up

As London opens (~11:00 Dubai), volume builds and gold often sets its daily direction.

Many traders look for the day's trend to establish here, then manage it into New York.

New York: prime time

The London–New York overlap (~16:30–20:00 Dubai) plus US data releases bring gold's biggest moves.

This is the main window for active gold traders — and the most dangerous for the undisciplined.

Your session plan

Pick one window that fits your life — e.g. two evening hours in Dubai covering the overlap — and ignore the rest.

Consistency of timing builds pattern recognition; random screen time builds confusion.

Key takeaways

  • Asia: quiet ranges. London: direction-setting. New York: biggest moves.
  • The 16:30–20:00 Dubai window is prime gold time.
  • Trade one consistent window instead of random hours.

Frequently asked questions

Can I trade gold in the Dubai morning?

You can watch, but expect slow conditions until London opens.

What about US news at 16:30 Dubai?

Major US data drops then — spreads widen and gold whips. Beginners should observe, not trade, the release minute.

Does gold trade on weekends?

No — like forex, it closes Friday evening UTC and reopens Sunday.
