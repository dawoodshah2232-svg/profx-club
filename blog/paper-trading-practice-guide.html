Paper Trading: Practice Before Real Money

September 23, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

Paper trading (demo trading) lets you trade real markets with virtual money. Used well, it is the safest classroom in trading.

What paper trading gives you

A demo account mirrors live prices, so you can practise placing orders, setting stop-losses, testing strategies and learning your platform's buttons without risking a dirham.

Every beginner should spend weeks here. Mistakes on demo cost nothing; the same mistakes live cost real money.

How to practise properly

Treat demo seriously: use the same account size you would really deposit, risk the same small percentages, and keep a journal of every trade.

If you 'play' with huge demo sizes, you learn nothing transferable. Realistic practice builds realistic habits.

The limits of demo

Demo cannot teach you the emotions of real money — fear and greed only arrive when cash is on the line.

Demo fills are also sometimes kinder than live fills. So treat demo as driving school: essential, but the real road still surprises you.

When to consider going live

Consider a small live account only after 2–3 months of consistent, journaled demo results following a written plan.

Start tiny — the goal of your first live months is learning to handle emotions, not making money.

Key takeaways

  • Demo with realistic sizes and journal every trade.
  • Demo builds skills, not emotional control — expect a gap.
  • Go live small, only after months of disciplined demo results.

Frequently asked questions

Are demo accounts free?

Yes — brokers offer them free, usually with virtual funds you can reset.

How long should I demo trade?

A common guideline is 2–3 months of consistent, plan-following results.

Can demo results guarantee live success?

No. They prove your process works mechanically; emotions are a separate challenge.
