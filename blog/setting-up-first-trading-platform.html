Setting Up Your First Trading Platform

August 12, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

Your first platform login can feel overwhelming. This walkthrough gets you from install to first demo trade calmly.

Install and log in

Download MT4/MT5 only from your broker's official site or official app stores.

Open your demo account credentials email, enter the server name exactly, and log in. Save credentials securely — never share them.

Arrange your workspace

Open one chart (EUR/USD, H1), switch to candlesticks, remove clutter indicators, and save the layout as a template.

Learn the three essential windows: Market Watch (prices), Navigator (accounts/indicators), Terminal (trades/history).

Place your first demo trade

Press F9 (or tap Trade), set 0.01 lots, add a stop-loss and take-profit, and execute.

Watch the Terminal tab show your floating profit/loss. Then close it and review — congratulations, you have completed the mechanics.

Learn the safety buttons

Know how to close a single trade, close all trades, and set alerts before you need them in a hurry.

Also enable one-click trading only when you are experienced — beginners misclick expensive buttons.

Key takeaways

  • Download only from official sources; guard your credentials.
  • Master one clean chart layout and the three key windows.
  • Practise stops, targets and emergency closes on demo first.

Frequently asked questions

How long until the platform feels natural?

A week or two of daily demo use for most people.

Should I enable one-click trading?

Not as a beginner — it makes accidental trades too easy.

What if I forget my investor vs master password?

Investor password is read-only; keep the master private and stored safely.
