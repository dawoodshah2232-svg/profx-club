Membership terms

The rules of free membership at ProFX Club.

1. Free membership

Membership is free. ProFX Club never requires a payment, deposit, broker account or trading activity to join or remain a member.

2. Eligibility

Members must provide a real name and a working email address. Accounts are for individuals.

3. Education, not advice

Classes, webinars and resources are educational. Nothing we publish is financial advice or a recommendation to trade.

4. No guaranteed outcomes

We never promise profits. Anyone claiming guaranteed returns in our name is not authorised by us.

5. Fair use

Members must not abuse bookings (e.g. bulk reservations), impersonate others, or misuse community spaces.

6. Paid extras

Travel, third-party professional services and selected physical activities may carry clearly disclosed charges. They are optional and never required.

7. Suspension & closure

We may suspend or close accounts that breach these terms or the community rules, after review.

8. Changes

We will publish a new version and notify members of material changes. Version: v0.1-demo (owner approval pending).
