Education risk notice

Understand the risks before you learn about trading.

1. Trading is risky

Forex and CFD trading involves substantial risk of loss. Most retail traders lose money.

2. Education only

ProFX Club provides education, not financial advice, signals or recommendations.

3. Hypothetical examples

Examples in classes are hypothetical and for illustration only — they are not predictions.

4. No guarantees

Past performance, backtests and examples do not guarantee future results. We never promise profits.

5. Your responsibility

Any trading decision is yours alone. Never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

6. Scam awareness

Beware of anyone promising guaranteed returns, 'risk-free' profits or asking for your passwords or funds — including anyone claiming to represent us.
