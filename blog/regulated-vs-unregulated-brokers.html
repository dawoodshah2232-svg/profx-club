Regulated vs Unregulated Brokers

August 19, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

Regulation is the difference between a supervised financial firm and a website with your money. Here is what it really means.

What regulation requires

Licensed brokers must segregate client funds from company money, meet capital requirements, report regularly, handle complaints fairly, and often participate in compensation schemes.

Regulators can fine or shut down violators. These are concrete protections.

What unregulated means

No segregation requirements, no conduct oversight, no compensation scheme, no one to complain to.

If the firm disappears with your deposit, you have essentially no recourse. Some unregulated brokers are honest; you cannot verify which.

The marketing tricks

Unregulated firms lure with extreme leverage (1:1000+), huge bonuses, and 'guaranteed profits'.

Regulated brokers cannot offer these precisely because regulators banned the traps. When the offer looks amazing, ask what protection you are trading away.

How to check in 2 minutes

Find the licence number on the broker's site, then search it on the regulator's official register.

Match the company name exactly — scammers clone real licence numbers onto fake firms. Two minutes now saves your entire deposit.

Key takeaways

  • Regulation = segregated funds, oversight, complaints process, compensation.
  • Unregulated = no verifiable protections and no recourse.
  • Verify licence numbers on the regulator's own register.

Frequently asked questions

Is a broker regulated in one country safe for me?

Generally yes if the regulator is reputable — check whether UAE clients fall under that entity.

What about brokers regulated in small island nations?

Weaker oversight than tier-one regulators. Treat with extra caution.

My broker claims regulation but I can't verify it — ?

Do not deposit. Unverifiable claims are a classic scam marker.
