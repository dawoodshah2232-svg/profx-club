Demo Accounts: How to Use Them Well

August 11, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

A demo account trades real prices with virtual money. Used realistically, it is the best free education in trading.

Set realistic conditions

Choose a demo balance matching what you would really deposit, use the same leverage, and trade the same hours.

A $100,000 demo teaches nothing to someone who will deposit $500 — the emotions and maths are entirely different.

Practise the full process

Don't just click buy/sell — write a mini plan per trade, set stops and targets, journal results, and review weekly.

Demo is for rehearsing your entire process, including the boring parts.

Know demo's blind spots

Fills can be kinder than live, and there is zero emotional pressure.

Expect live trading to feel harder even with identical mechanics. Demo proves your process works; it cannot prove your psychology will hold.

Graduation criteria

Consider demo 'complete' after 2–3 months of following your plan with consistent, journaled results — not after one lucky week.

Then go live with the smallest size possible. The first live goal is emotional education, not profit.

Key takeaways

  • Mirror your real future deposit, leverage and hours on demo.
  • Rehearse the full process: plan, execute, journal, review.
  • Graduate after months of disciplined results — then start live tiny.

Frequently asked questions

Are demo accounts really free?

Yes — brokers provide them free, typically with resettable virtual funds.

Can I reset my demo balance?

Usually yes, through your broker's client portal or by opening a fresh demo.

How is demo different from live?

Same prices, but easier fills and no real emotions. Treat it as rehearsal, not proof.
