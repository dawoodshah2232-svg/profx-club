Forex Market Hours: When to Trade

September 26, 2026 · 2 min read · By ProFX Club

Forex never sleeps on weekdays — but not all hours are equal. Liquidity and volatility rise and fall as the world's trading sessions open and close.

The four sessions

The market rotates through Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York. Tokyo brings Asian flows, London brings the biggest volume of the day, and New York overlaps with London for the busiest hours.

Sydney is the quietest. In Dubai (GMT+4), London opens around 11:00 and New York around 16:30.

The golden overlap

The London–New York overlap (roughly 16:30–20:00 Dubai time) is when spreads are tightest and moves are cleanest.

Most day traders focus their screen time here. Outside these hours, markets can drift sideways and chop up short-term positions.

When to stay away

Late Friday, thin holiday periods and the minutes around major news are treacherous: spreads widen and prices can gap.

Beginners do best with a fixed trading window — for example, two focused hours during the overlap — rather than staring at charts all day.

Weekends

Forex closes on weekends (roughly Friday 22:00 to Sunday 22:00 UTC).

Prices can gap on Sunday's open after weekend news, which is why carrying large positions into the weekend is risky for beginners.

Key takeaways

  • The London–New York overlap has the best liquidity (evenings in Dubai).
  • Trade a fixed window; avoid news spikes and dead hours.
  • Weekend gaps make holding big positions over weekends risky.

Frequently asked questions

What time does forex open in Dubai?

The week starts around 01:00–02:00 Monday Dubai time (Sunday 22:00 UTC), with the first real liquidity in the Tokyo session.

Can I trade at night in the UAE?

The New York session runs into the Dubai evening, so yes — many UAE traders focus on 16:30–21:00.

Is 24-hour trading an advantage?

Only if you are selective. Being able to trade anytime tempts beginners into overtrading.
