How booking works

Fair booking, for everyone.

One place per member

Every member can book one place per event. Capacity is enforced — full events can't be overbooked.

Waitlist when full

Full events keep a waitlist. If someone cancels, the first waitlisted member is promoted automatically.

Cancel anytime

Cancelling releases your place immediately for someone else. Online joining links go only to registered attendees.
